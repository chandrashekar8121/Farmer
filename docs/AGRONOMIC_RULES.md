# Krishi Saathi - Agronomic Standards & Fertilizer Calculation Reference

## 1. Regulatory & Scientific Source
All nutrient thresholds and fertilizer recommendation formulas in Krishi Saathi follow published standards from:
- **Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)** - *Soil Testing in India: Methods and Interpretation Manual*
- **Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)** - *Crop Production Guide (Agriculture)*
- **Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt. of India** - *Soil Health Card Scheme Norms*
- **Department of Fertilizers, Govt. of India** - *Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) MRP notification for subsidized fertilizers (Urea ₹266.50/45kg, DAP ₹1350/50kg, MOP ₹1700/50kg)*

---

## 2. Soil Nutrient Ratings (mg/kg or ppm)
| Nutrient | Low | Medium | High |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nitrogen (N)** | < 125 mg/kg (< 280 kg/ha) | 125 – 250 mg/kg (280 – 560 kg/ha) | > 250 mg/kg (> 560 kg/ha) |
| **Phosphorus (P)** | < 10 mg/kg (< 23 kg/ha P2O5) | 10 – 25 mg/kg (23 – 57 kg/ha) | > 25 mg/kg (> 57 kg/ha) |
| **Potassium (K)** | < 110 mg/kg (< 130 kg/ha K2O) | 110 – 280 mg/kg (130 – 335 kg/ha) | > 280 mg/kg (> 335 kg/ha) |

### Soil pH Interpretation:
- `< 5.5`: Strongly Acidic → Recommend agricultural lime (200–400 kg/acre)
- `5.5 – 6.5`: Slightly Acidic → Tolerable for most crops; add organic matter
- `6.5 – 7.5`: Neutral (Optimal nutrient availability)
- `7.5 – 8.5`: Moderately Alkaline → Prefer SSP and acid-forming fertilizers
- `> 8.5`: Strongly Alkaline / Sodic → Recommend agricultural gypsum (300–500 kg/acre)

---

## 3. Recommended Dose of Fertilizers (RDF kg/ha) & Adjustments
Soil test crop response (STCR) principle:
- **Low Nutrient Level**: Increase RDF by **+25%**
- **Medium Nutrient Level**: Apply **100%** of standard RDF
- **High Nutrient Level**: Decrease RDF by **-25%**

### Crop Baseline RDF (N : P2O5 : K2O kg/ha):
- **Rice (Paddy)**: 120 : 60 : 40
- **Wheat**: 120 : 60 : 40
- **Maize**: 120 : 60 : 40
- **Cotton**: 120 : 60 : 60
- **Sugarcane**: 250 : 100 : 120
- **Groundnut**: 25 : 50 : 75
- **Tomato**: 150 : 100 : 100
- **Chilli**: 120 : 60 : 60
- **Chickpea / Pulses**: 20 : 40 : 20

---

## 4. Land Area Conversions
- 1 Hectare = 2.47105 Acres
- 1 Acre = 4046.86 Square Metres (0.404686 Hectare)
- Standard rate per Acre = Rate per Hectare / 2.47105

---

## 5. Fertilizer Product Composition
- **Neem Coated Urea**: 46% N (0.46 kg N per kg Urea)
- **DAP (Di-Ammonium Phosphate)**: 18% N, 46% P2O5
- **MOP (Muriate of Potash)**: 60% K2O
- **SSP (Single Super Phosphate)**: 16% P2O5, 11% Sulfur

---

## 6. Worked Test Example Calculation
**Scenario:**
- Crop: **Rice (Paddy)**
- Field Area: **2 Acres** (0.80937 Hectare)
- Soil Test: N = 100 mg/kg (Low), P = 18 mg/kg (Medium), K = 150 mg/kg (Medium), pH = 6.8 (Neutral)

**Step 1: Nutrient Adjustments**
- N: Low → 120 * 1.25 = 150 kg/ha N
- P: Medium → 60 * 1.00 = 60 kg/ha P2O5
- K: Medium → 40 * 1.00 = 40 kg/ha K2O

**Step 2: Nutrient Requirement for 2 Acres (0.80937 ha)**
- Target N = 150 * 0.80937 = 121.41 kg N
- Target P2O5 = 60 * 0.80937 = 48.56 kg P2O5
- Target K2O = 40 * 0.80937 = 32.37 kg K2O

**Step 3: Fertilizer Quantities**
1. **DAP for Phosphorus**:
   - DAP needed = 48.56 / 0.46 = 105.57 kg (~2.11 bags of 50kg)
   - Nitrogen provided by this DAP = 105.57 * 0.18 = 19.00 kg N
2. **Urea for remaining Nitrogen**:
   - Remaining N = 121.41 - 19.00 = 102.41 kg N
   - Urea needed = 102.41 / 0.46 = 222.63 kg (~4.95 bags of 45kg)
3. **MOP for Potassium**:
   - MOP needed = 32.37 / 0.60 = 53.95 kg (~1.08 bags of 50kg)

**Step 4: Estimated Cost (Govt. Subsidized MRP)**
- DAP: 105.57 kg @ (₹1350 / 50kg = ₹27.00/kg) = ₹2,850.39
- Urea: 222.63 kg @ (₹266.50 / 45kg = ₹5.92/kg) = ₹1,317.97
- MOP: 53.95 kg @ (₹1700 / 50kg = ₹34.00/kg) = ₹1,834.30
- **Total Estimated Cost**: **₹6,002.66**
